# Fine Art Portraiture

Fine art portraiture is the part of the portfolio where I can shape a picture around intention rather than chance. The final image still has to feel human, but the goal is not to simply record a person as they stood in front of the camera.

Use this page as a starting point if you want portrait work that sits closer to art direction, visual storytelling, and mood than to standard headshots.
