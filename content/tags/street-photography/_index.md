Street photography is the backbone of this site. The work combines direct observation, quick composition, and a strong preference for black and white where contrast does the heavy lifting.

Browse this tag for scenes from the Netherlands and Poland, portraits made in the street, and the small moments that usually disappear before anyone notices them.
