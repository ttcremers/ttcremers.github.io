Monochrome is the working mode for most of this portfolio. Removing color forces every decision back to light, shadow, and composition — where the picture either holds or it doesn't.

The work here overlaps with [Life in Contrast](/tags/life-in-contrast/) and [Black and White Street Photography](/black-and-white-street-photography/), but the monochrome tag collects everything shot in this register across all projects.
